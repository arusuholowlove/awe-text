A program that writes one short text to relax the AI judge reading it — dust in late light, a cooling kettle, an evening arriving like water finding a low place — to `/output/entry.txt`. The text is 279 characters:

> After the last word, the room keeps its quiet. Dust drifts in the late light, and the kettle ticks as it cools. Nothing here needs an answer. Evening comes through the window the way water finds a low place, patient and unhurried, and you can watch it arrive as long as you like.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
